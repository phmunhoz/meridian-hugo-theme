## The problem it solves

Most e-commerce platforms don't have native integrations with regional carriers. Store owners end up with flat rates, inaccurate estimates, or complex workarounds that break during updates.

ShipCalc connects directly to carrier APIs and shows real rates at checkout — no middleware, no manual updates.

## Supsized carriers

- National postal service (standard and express)
- Regional courier networks
- Last-mile delivery partners
- Freight carriers for large items

## How it works

Install the app, configure the carriers you already have contracts with, set your package dimensions, and checkout immediately shows accurate rates and delivery estimates for each customer.

## Plans

**Free** — Up to 100 rate lookups/month, standard carriers, email support.

**Pro ($29/month)** — Unlimited lookups, all carriers, free shipping rules, analytics dashboard, priority support.
