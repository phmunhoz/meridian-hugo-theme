## Ongoing partnership, not reactive support

I don't work as a help desk where you open a ticket and wait. The idea is a close working relationship where I understand the business deeply enough to anticipate problems and opportunities.

Most clients who start with maintenance and evolution naturally expand the engagement — new integrations, feature development, and often a move to the enterprise tier when volume justifies it.
