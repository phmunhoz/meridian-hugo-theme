## Why migrate?

Most platforms that made sense when you started become a liability as you scale. The real cost isn't just the license — it's the team time, the agency dependency, and the months between a decision and its execution.

A well-planned migration typically delivers:

- **30–70% reduction** in annual platform costs
- **Same-day changes** instead of weeks waiting on developers
- **Zero downtime** during the cutover, with full operational continuity
